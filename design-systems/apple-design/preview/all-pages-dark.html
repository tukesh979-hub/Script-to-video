Apple Design — Dark (Liquid Glass). Reframed, not reskinned: floating Dynamic-Island-style pill nav instead of a full-width bar, a bento-grid dashboard instead of hero+section stacking, a floating vertical dock instead of an edge-anchored sidebar, How It Works as a spatial bento grid instead of a list. Glass is much stronger this time — 42–46px blur + 200% saturation + a bright top-edge highlight, sitting over vivid color blooms (blue/purple/pink) so the blur has real color to refract, not a flat wash. Corners are sharp and pointed (0 radius) throughout, per explicit instruction — the only exceptions are genuinely circular elements (avatar, status dots, the send button), which stay round because they're circles by function, not cornered panels.
